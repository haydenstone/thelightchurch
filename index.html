I · Our Mission

Christ at the Center.

A Christ-centered community dedicated to proclaiming the Gospel through worship, teaching, fellowship, compassion, and service.

Faith Made Living

Our purpose is not merely to speak about faith, but to live it. We seek to glorify Christ through how we worship, how we serve, how we forgive, and how we care for one another.

Scripture remains our foundation, grace our invitation, and Christ our center.
